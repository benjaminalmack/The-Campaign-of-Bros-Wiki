# The Golden Concord
Largest merchant guild in the world.  Ships were seen to the north of the island being escorted by heavy frigates.  

# Mysterious Earthquakes
Earthquakes periodically shake Stormwreck Isle.

# Winkus
Winkus was born in the streets of Neverwinter as an orphan.  He wanted to make it big in the music industry of Neverwinter but was told that his ballads were not inspired.  He wonder the world, seeking inspiration to become Neverwinter's next big super star.

# Sparkrender's Ritual
Sparkrender was performing a ritual to summon the powers of the dragons buried under Stormwreck Isle
