# Jerry of Thundertree

Your parents lived in the prosperous village of Thundertree, east of
the city of Neverwinter and at the edge of the Neverwinter Wood. But
when nearby Mount Hotenow erupted thirty years ago, your parents
fled, perhaps carrying you in your infancy (depending on how old you
are). Your family drifted from village to village around the region, find-
ing work as laborers where they could.

Your background shaped your character in important ways. You
learned the languages of several different peoples (shown in “Lan-
guages” on the front of this sheet). Your skill proficiencies in Animal
Handling and Survival also reflect your upbringing, working with ani-
mals and getting by in the natural world.

You’ve spent the last few years in Neverwinter as a carpenter work-
ing at the city’s bustling docks. But it’s clear to you and everyone
around you that you are destined for much more. You stood up to an
abusive ship captain once, so other dockworkers look up to you. Some-
day, you’ll come into your own. You’ll be a hero.

Personal Goal: Determine Your Destiny. In the remote cloister
of Dragon’s Rest lives an old sage who is supposed to possess great
wisdom—or possibly supernatural insight. The cloister holds a temple
to the dragon god Bahamut, who is a patron of heroes and a champion
of justice. Maybe Elder Runara can help you determine exactly what
your heroic destiny is and set you on the right path to fulfill it, so you
can become the hero you know you’re meant to be.


# Levi Jaegor

You defeat a monster in my small town, you become the local hero. Defeat five in the span of a year, you are labeled "The Hero of Ages".  

The high priests, however, consider me a threat. According to scripture, I am the bridge to allow these vicious creatures to enter our lands that have been free of monsters for a century.  

I can't help but think they're right.  

No longer can I wait for evil to continue to wreak havoc in my hometown of Marshwood. If my instincts prove me correct, I must journey beyond the seas to find who I am, or, what I am.


# Mizer of Graystone

Mizer of Graystone, a Tiefling wizard, was born into a family of influential merchants and rose to political power through cunning and dark magic learned from an ancient tome dedicated to Mask, the god of thieves and shadows. Despite his success, Mizer's empire fell due to betrayal and rival conspiracies, reducing him to a commoner. His unwavering devotion to Mask, who promises restoration and vengeance through conquest, drives Mizer to seek ancient artifacts and forbidden knowledge to reclaim his lost power and exact revenge on those who wronged him

# Onyx the Conflicted

I was raised by a noble family to be a leader and a fighter. I was raised that honor is the most important trait that anyone can hold. When Barbarians took our homeland and captured our family, I had to throw everything I knew behind me in order to save them. Deceive, lie, cheat, steal, stab others in the back, not fighting opponents head on (from the shadows). It was not something I wanted to do, but I was able to save my family and begin to rebuild. In the process of saving my family, I was able to gain favor in certain criminal organizations and common folk. In the same way, I now have mixed favor with some noble houses and distant family, but remain in good standing with close family and loyal houses to my own.
