# Stormwreck Isle
The island is mountainous. It is a beacon to dragons, and has become suffused with the magic of countless dragons who had died there. It is said that this has left a spiritual wound across the island that could cause unpredictable magical phenomena.

The rocky island was characterized by basalt cliffs, sea caves, and hot springs. Sharp rocks along the coast were the cause of many shipwrecks, especially along a particularly treacherous shoal to the northwest that was strewn with massive gold dragon bones. On the southwest side of the island was Seagrow Caves, where toxic fumes said to originate in the volcanic tomb of the red dragon Sharruth vented to the surface.
