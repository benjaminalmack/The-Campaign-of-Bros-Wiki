Your party looks toward the edge of the cliff where the magical walkway leads off toward the distant horizon. But as your eyes follow the path, you see something—a shadow cast over the walkway, large and ominous. The air around the cliff seems to shimmer, warping with a heat that doesn't match the coolness of the Observatory's interior.
A creature, its three heads raised high, snarls from the opposite cliffside. Its lion head, eyes blazing with malice, roars, causing the ground beneath your feet to tremble. The goat head bleats, the sound a twisted, unnatural sound. And the serpent's head hisses, its tongue flicking out as it locks eyes with your party.
The Chimera begins to move, its hulking form slowly stalking toward the magical bridge between the Rotunda and the cliff. The stone beneath its claws crumbles slightly with each step, as if even the ancient structure cannot withstand its presence.
The creature’s approach is slow but relentless. You have only a few moments to decide: will you face the beast, or try to find another way to escape this new threat?
