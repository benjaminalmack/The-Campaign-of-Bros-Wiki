# Chapter 1: Arrival on Stormwreck Isle & The Myconids

Mizer, Onyx, Bob and Jerry came to the Stormwreck Isle for different reasons but ultimately united together in the spirit of adventure.  You fucked your way through the spore octopus and the Myconids, acting as diplomats for Tarak to mend relations between Dragon's Rest and the Myconid people of Stormwreck Isle.

Levi Jaegor and Reid the Weed came on the next boat to Stormwreck Isle, connecting with Mizer, Onyx, Bob and Jerry after a bout with zombies on the docks of Dragon’s Rest.  Levi and Reid, enamored by the spirit of adventure, joined them in their exploration of Stormwreck Isle.  


# Chapter 2: Choices & Consequences in Dragon's Rest

As they were getting to know the townspeople of Dragon's Rest, they came up with a plan to kidnap Varnoth, not appreciating her callous nature. However, in planning for this kidnapping, Rix, a Kobold with close relations to the leader of Stormwreck Isle, heard it and informed Runara, the leader of Stormwreck isle of the issue.  Our characters, realizing their reputation on the island was at stake, decided to capture Rix.  During this tense hostage situation, Reid attempted to kill Rix.  Just before Rix passed into the great beyond, a bronze dragon burst through the door of the Kitchen Area of Dragon's Rest, where Rix was being kept, to save the Kobold from Reid’s wrath.

The bronze dragon reveals herself to be Runara, and the characters discover she was a dragon all along.  Runara requests the group atone for their actions by helping to heal the curse that has befallen the island.


# Chapter 3: Lifting the Island's Curse

In journeying to the potential source of the curse, an abandoned crashed ship called the Compass Rose, the group defeated several fume drakes at the Hot Springs and acquired wind spores, which provided them the ability to breath underwater.

Once the group arrived at the Compass Rose, they decided to make their way through the ship via a hole at the bottom of the ship (for which they called "the gaping hole"), utilizing their wind spores to help them breath underwater while navigating the lowest level of the Compass Rose.  Making their way through the ship, a tense battle ensued, with Reid and Levi almost getting themselves killed.

After defeating the zombies and the ghoulish, white creature, the group found a curious talisman and a [[9. Special Campaign Items#Letter from Compass Rose|letter]] in Compass Rose’s hold, which appeared to be linked to the island's curse.  The group then valiantly defeated a pair of Harpies before figuring out how to fulfill Runara's request to save the island.

After returning to the main part of the island from the Compass Rose, the group runs into and viciously defeats an Owlbear, Mizer showing no mercy to what appeared to be an innocent creature surrendering to them.  

Following the request from the letter from the Compass Rose, the group returned the Talisman of Aleitha to the grave of her husband, Brathos.  The burying of the talisman appeared have lifted the curse of Compass Rose on the island.

# Chapter 4: The Blue Dragon's Ritual

Just as everything seems to be calming down, you see a large lighting strike appears on the southwest side of the mountain and you hear the violent screech of a large animal.  The ground shakes beneath you like it did when you were on the compass rose.  You decide to take action and venture towards this unknown calamity.

On the way, you befriended Winkus Thricebound, a cheeky gnome bard from the city of Neverwinter.  Winkus decided to join you on your adventures to gather inspiration for his ballads, despite Reid and Mizer brutally mind raping him to figure out his [[4. NPCs#Winkus Thricebound of Neverwinter|background]].

Making your way around the coast of the island, the group absolutely obliterated 10 Kobolds with back to back critical hits.  Even with Levi Jaegor sitting on his ass the whole time looking for four leaf clovers, the rest of the adventurers pushed forward and paved the way to the southwest part of the island

When the group got to what appears to be an Observatory, you observe some type of ritual taking place by a blue wyrmling dragon named Sparkrender and his Kobold minions.  Sparkrender appeared to have captured a bronze dragon, Lord Aidron, a student of Runara's, who was being used as a sacrifice for this ritual.  The group, assuming the blue dragon was up to no good,  decided to take on this foe. Sparkrender’s lightning was almost too much for our heroes, but, luckily, Jerry of Thundertree’s aim is always true and was able to mortally wound Sparkrender after a perfect shot to the chest while Sparkrender was mid-flight.  Sparkrender would flee in shame.  

During the battle, Lord Aidron, in order to save the group, decided to take Sparkrender's place and redirected the ritual at himself, transforming him from a dragon Wyrmling to a full sized adult dragon.  After the battle, the group licks its wounds and decides to rest at the Observatory overnight with Lord Aidron.

You all awake in the morning to Lord Aidron pacing about.  He speaks to the group, “You have done what none could, and yet I feel no gratitude.  You saved me, but I don't yet understand what I have unleashed. The Ancestors’ power is within me, and I do not know what it will cost. I am not the wyrmling I once was. The power… it pulses through my veins, and I feel both reborn and condemned.  What is my purpose now?  I have much to ponder, and I am afraid I must leave you."

# Chapter 5: The Three Headed Monster & Levi's Past

After Lord Aidron flies way, your party looks toward the edge of the cliff where a creature, its three heads raised high, snarls from the opposite cliffside. Its lion head, eyes blazing with malice, roars, causing the ground beneath your feet to tremble. The goat head bleats, the sound a twisted, unnatural sound. And the serpent's head hisses, its tongue flicking out as it locks eyes with your party.

The creature seemed like it could be too much for the group, but, luckily, Deus Ex Winkus decided to step up for the first time to be a part of the ballad instead of writing it.  With some bardic inspiration from our favorite gnome, the group gained advantage over this beast before it was defeated.  Before this creature fled the scene in defeat, it calls out Levi, cursing his family and vowing revenge.

# Chapter 6: Hunted

Suspicion has been raised about Levi's [[3. Your Characters#Levi Jaegor|background]].  Levi confesses his history with monsters and how they always seem to follow him.  Before you are able to get to the bottom of this mystery, the group still appears to be in danger. From somewhere just beyond the mountains, a sound rises—low, wet, and wrong.  It is not a howl.  It is the sound of something calling a name it does not yet speak. 

All of a sudden, from below the cliff face, a lantern flickers.  Tarak's voice cuts through the win “Oi! You lot alive up there?”.  He appears to have heard your battles while venturing to the Seagrow Caves and wanted to check out what the commotion was about.  His eyes narrow as he hears the distant sound.  “That… that’s not somethin’ that belongs here.”

The group decides to jump off the cliff into the sea and escape with Tarak in his boat back to Dragon's Rest.  However, as the group continues to navigate around the coast of the island,   glowing eyes track the boat from the cliffs above, not with rage, but with calculation.

When the group finally makes it to Dragon's Rest, Varnoth greets them and appears to be very tense.  She confirms that the creature arrived asking about a specific man.  It spoke of “debts unpaid” and “the Hunt.”  Varnoth takes the back to her place in Dragon's Rest and reveals her a bit about her [[4. NPCs#Varnoth|past]] and the [[4. NPCs#Varnoth's Past with the Barghest|specific experience]] she has with this create that is called a Barghest.  The group, not knowing what they are getting into, request Varnoth provide assistance. Varnoth agrees to help them, not out of love for the group, but because the island is supposed to be a place for refuge and knows what this creature is capable of.    Varnoth tells the group to meet back at her place around sunset and will go over how to defeat this creature. 

# Chapter 7: The Golden Concord's Quest

Waiting for Varnoth to gather resources for her plan to defeat the Barghest, our group meets with newcomers in Dragon's Rest, two representatives from the Golden Concord, a major merchant guild,  The guild is looking for sellswords to help them escort their ship to Neverwinter and protect it from New Hope Island pirates.  Their ships carry gold, silver, copper, diamonds, and magic myconid mushrooms.  The group can be paid in coins or in any of the materials carried on the ship.  The group accepts the quest.  The Golden Concords informs them they plan to leave the island in 3 days and tells the group to meet them at the docks then.  

# Chapter 8: The Kitchen Debacle

After accepting a quest from the Golden Concord, the group goes to the Kitchen to sit down at the table for sustenance before they do battle with the Barghest.  Things escalate quickly though, as Mizer gets up to make himself some drinks at the bar and decides to send some mixed signals to a Kobold named Zark sitting next to him.  Mizer, not afraid of taking some risks, decides to tongue Zark, and Zark tongues him back.  

Seeing the scene unfold, a couple members of the group start getting into Mizer's raunchy escapade, with the Bob the Well Endowed cheering for more and Onyx touching himself under the table.  On the other hand, Jerry becomes overwhelmed by what is unfolding and becomes sick to his stomach.  Jerry runs into the back of the kitchen to find a place to throw-up.  Bursting into the back area to find a place to throw up, Jerry startles the Kobold Rix who happened to also be in the back area of the kitch, still in crutches from the injuries caused by Reid the other day.  

Despite what appeared to be just playful displays of public sexual debauchery, Mizer intentions take a turn for the worst as he uses a blow dart to knock Zark unconscious.  Just as Mizer is finished betraying Zarks trust, another Kobold, Mumpo, walk into the kitchen snd witnessed the startling scene.  Onyx, realizing how suspect this also looks, attempts to knock out Mumpo.  Mumpo easily dodges Onyx's punch and turns towards the exit of the Kitchen.
